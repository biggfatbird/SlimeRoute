The bundled `rail_lines_raster_0p2deg.npy` is a **derived 0.2-degree occupancy raster** from third-party mapped railway LineString geometry (datta07/INDIAN-SHAPEFILES, MIT license). It is genuine mapped-data-derived input but **not the full source track geometry** and not an official or current operational inventory. `research/build_rail_raster.py` regenerates the raster from an embedded encoding, NOT independently from the original downloaded GIS. To independently reconstruct it from full source, follow `research/download_real_tracks.py` and `research/rasterize_external_tracks.py --verify`. A future source mismatch must be investigated. Approximate country boundary and synthetic terrain remain in the simulation. Train stopping sequences are not physical track polylines.
